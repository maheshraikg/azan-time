Azan Time – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Azan Time shows prayer times, the Qibla direction, duas and a Hijri calendar. It is built to work offline and to keep everything on your phone.

Data we collect

None. Azan Time has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app has no internet permission and sends nothing to us or to anyone else.

Location

If you allow it, the app reads your location to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction for your place. The location is used and stored only on your phone. To show the name of your place, the app may ask your phone's built-in place-name service (Android's geocoder), which is provided by your phone's system, not by Azan Time. You can also choose your place by hand and keep location off.

Data stored on your phone

Your settings, chosen place, prayer tracker, tasbih counts and any timetable or sound file you import are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. They are removed when you uninstall the app.

Permissions

  • Location – prayer times and Qibla for where you are.
  • Notifications, alarms, full-screen alerts – the Azan and Iqama alerts at the right time, also on the lock screen.
  • Foreground service – keeps the next-prayer notification shown and plays the Azan.
  • Do Not Disturb access (optional) – silences the phone during prayers if you turn that on.
  • Motion sensors – the Qibla compass.

Sharing

When you share the prayer-times poster, the image and text go only to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp), and only when you tap Share.

Children

The app is suitable for everyone and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
